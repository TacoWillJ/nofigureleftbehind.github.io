In the grim darkness of the far future, Games Workshop removes datasheets. Units vanish between editions. Models gather dust. Armies are abandoned.

This project refuses that fate. Every unit that ever had rules deserves to still have rules. These are fan-made homebrew conversions — lovingly crafted, playtested, and offered freely to the community.

The No Figure Left Behind Tracker

What Is This?

A comprehensive spreadsheet tracking every Warhammer 40,000 unit across all eleven editions — from Rogue Trader to present day. Each unit is marked with its status in every edition it existed.

Where a unit has been removed, a homebrew datasheet card has been created to bring it back into 10th Edition rules, fully balanced against the current game.

This is a living document, maintained by fans for fans. No commercial interest. No affiliation with Games Workshop.

Legend

  • YES Fully supported in this edition
  • L Legends — rules exist, not tournament legal
  • X Removed — no rules in this edition
  • HB Homebrew — fan-made card created here
  • ? Uncertain — inferred, needs verification
  • M Merged — rolled into another datasheet
  • NA Rules existed but no model released
